# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 20, 2026

## Introduction

AlexTool is a privacy-focused web browser for Android. We believe that what you browse is your business, not ours. This Privacy Policy explains what information AlexTool collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information We Collect

AlexTool is designed to collect **as little information as possible**. By default, the app does **not** transmit your browsing data to any server owned or operated by the app author.

### Local storage only

The following data is stored **only on your device** and is never sent anywhere unless you explicitly choose to share it:

- Browsing history, bookmarks, and open tabs
- Saved passwords and login credentials (encrypted locally)
- Download history and scheduled downloads
- App settings and preferences
- Crash and debug logs (stored locally until you voluntarily send them)

### What we do not collect

- We do not track the websites you visit
- We do not sell or share your data with third parties
- We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs
- We do not collect location data
- We do not require an account or any personal registration

## Permissions

AlexTool requests the following Android permissions, each used only for the feature it enables:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Internet | Loading web pages (core browser function) |
| Storage / Files | Saving downloads and allowing you to pick save locations |
| Camera / Microphone | Used by websites you visit (e.g., video calls), only when you grant access |
| Notifications | Showing download progress |

You can revoke any permission at any time from Android Settings.

## Crash Reports

If a crash occurs, a local crash log is saved on your device. Nothing is sent automatically. You may choose to review and send the report through the "Debug & Crash Reports" screen, which opens a GitHub Issue on your behalf. You control exactly what is shared.

## Third-Party Services

AlexTool uses the Android System WebView to render web pages. Content you visit is served by third-party websites, which have their own privacy policies that we do not control. Use features like Incognito Mode and the built-in ad/content filtering to reduce tracking from websites.

## Children

AlexTool includes optional content-filtering features (e.g., Quiver Guard) that parents can enable. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions are bundled with app releases and are always viewable in-app from **Settings → About → Privacy Policy**.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, reach out via the Telegram channel linked in **Settings → About**.
